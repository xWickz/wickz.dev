**Short answer:** a website for a small business costs between **150 and 1,500 USD** from a freelance developer, depending on what it needs. A landing page starts at about 250 USD, a web catalog with a cart and WhatsApp at about 400 USD, and a custom web app at about 1,200 USD. On top of that you pay for the domain, about 10 to 15 USD a year.

These are my reference prices. Below I explain what each one includes and what makes the price go up or down.

| Type of website | Starting at | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|

| [Landing page](/en/services/landing-pages/) | 250 USD | One service, one product or a campaign |

All of them include a phone-friendly design, Google Search Console setup, publishing and connecting your domain.

## What makes the price go up

- **Amount of content.** Loading 30 products isn't the same as loading 400 with photos, prices and categories.
- **Extra features.** Search, filters, a cart, a panel to edit products, online payments or user logins.
- **Integrations.** Connecting to an inventory system, a spreadsheet or an external API.
- **Copy and photos.** If you have them ready, the work goes faster. If they need writing or editing, that adds up.

## What you pay afterwards

| Item | Approximate cost |
|---|---|
| Domain (`yourbusiness.com`) | 10 to 15 USD a year |
| Hosting for a static site | 0 to 20 USD a month |
| Maintenance (changes, updates) | Optional, 50 to 100 USD a month depending on the project |

A static site, with no admin panel or exposed database, needs almost no maintenance. It's one of the reasons I usually recommend them for landing pages and catalogs: you pay once and it works.

## How not to overpay

1. **Make sure the domain is in your name.** It's the most expensive and most common mistake: if the domain belongs to the developer, the website isn't really yours.
2. **Start with what sells.** A well-made landing page or catalog brings more customers than a ten-section site nobody reads.
3. **Look at real results, not just designs.** Ask what happened to the business. With [a PC hardware store's catalog](/en/case-studies/hardware-store-catalog/), for example, customers started arriving ready to buy and the owner stopped sending price lists by hand.
4. **Be wary of too cheap.** A 50 USD template usually ends up slow, poorly ranked on Google and hard to change.
