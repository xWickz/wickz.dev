## Why move your menu online

A printed menu has two problems: reprinting costs money and it's always out of date. When a price goes up or a new dish comes out, the usual fix is crossing things out, sticking on a label or telling customers in person. With a digital menu you change it once and everyone sees it, at the table and outside the restaurant.

It also answers a question that arrives on WhatsApp every day: "can you send me the menu?". Instead of sending photos of the menu, you send a link.

## What's included

Using the Don Chaker demo as an example:

- **Categories** so customers go straight to what they want: deals, empanadas, arepas, breakfast, chicken, red meat, fish, pasta, burgers, Middle Eastern food, sandwiches, salads, snacks, coffee and drinks.
- **Dish search**, useful when the menu is long.
- **A description for each dish**, with ingredients and portion size where relevant (for example, "150 g shawarma with fresh vegetables and sauces").
- **Visible prices**, including the reference prices commonly used in Venezuela.
- **A deals section** at the top, for what you want to sell first.
- **Fast loading on phones**, even on mobile data.

## How the QR code works

The QR code only stores the menu's address. That's why it's generated once and can be printed on tables, at the entrance or at the counter. Anything you change later in the menu (dishes, prices, deals) shows up when people scan the same code.
