## When you need a landing page

- You're about to spend on ads and want every visit to have one clear option.
- You're launching a product, a course, a promotion or an event.
- You want to capture leads (bookings, sign-ups, appointments) without building a full website.
- Your business offers one main thing and you'd rather explain it well on a single page.

## What's included

- **A clear main message** at the very top: what you offer and who it's for.
- **A single action**, repeated where it makes sense: message on WhatsApp, submit a form or book.
- **Proof that builds trust**, such as results, case studies or real contact details.
- **Mobile-first design**, because that's where most ad traffic comes from.
- **Fast loading**, without heavy libraries or unoptimized images.
- **Search-friendly title and description**, so the link also looks good when shared.
- **Click tracking** on the main buttons, if you want to know what works.
