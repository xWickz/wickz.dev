## When you need custom development

- Your business has a manual process (sign-ups, orders, reports) that eats hours every week.
- You need users to log in and see or manage their own data.
- You want to connect several tools: forms, databases, email, WhatsApp or external services.
- You have a product idea and need a working first version to test it.

## How I work

1. **First conversation.** What problem the app solves, who uses it and what's essential for the first version.
2. **Proposal.** Scope, technologies and phases. If something can be solved more simply, I'll tell you.
3. **Building in steps.** I show you progress you can try, instead of waiting until the end to see the result.
4. **Launch.** The app goes live, on its domain and ready to use.

## Simple architecture

Most projects don't need a complicated architecture. I prefer solutions that are easy to understand and maintain: fewer moving parts, fewer dependencies and clear code. That makes future changes cheaper, whether I make them or another developer does.

If your project is smaller, like presenting a product or capturing leads, a [landing page](/en/services/landing-pages/) may be enough.
