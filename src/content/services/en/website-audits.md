## When an audit makes sense

- Your site is slow, especially on phones.
- You get few or no visits from Google and don't know why.
- You're about to redesign or spend on ads and want to fix the basics first.
- You inherited a site from someone else and don't know what state it's in.
- You want a second opinion before hiring a developer or agency.

## What's included

- **Speed and Core Web Vitals:** real measurements on mobile, with the heaviest images, scripts and fonts called out.
- **Technical SEO:** titles, descriptions, headings, indexing, sitemap, redirects and structured data.
- **Security basics:** HTTPS, security headers, outdated software and exposed files. See also [how to tell if a website is secure](/en/blog/is-your-website-secure/).
- **Mobile and accessibility basics:** tap targets, contrast, readable text and keyboard use.
- **A prioritized action list:** high impact and low effort first.

## How I work

1. You send me the address of your site and what you want it to achieve.
2. I review it and measure it the way Google and your visitors see it.
3. I send a written report, ranked by impact, in plain language.
4. If you want, I fix the issues, or I hand the list to your own developer.

If the audit shows the site needs a rebuild, see [custom web development](/en/services/full-stack-web-development/) or [landing pages](/en/services/landing-pages/).
