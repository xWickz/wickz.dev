## Who it's for

A web catalog makes sense when you answer "how much?" many times a day, or when your products don't fit in a few Instagram stories. For example:

- Tech, auto parts, clothing or cosmetics shops that sell through Instagram and WhatsApp.
- Businesses with many products or prices that change often.
- Distributors who currently send price lists as PDFs or loose photos on WhatsApp.

## What's included

What you see in the demos is what I can build for your business:

- **Products by category.** In the laptop demo, for example: Intel, Ryzen, Gaming and Open Box.
- **A page for each product** with photo, specs and price.
- **Search, filters and sort by price** for larger catalogs, as in WickzTech and WTECH.
- **A WhatsApp button** so customers can reach you without copying your number.
- **Built for phones**, which is where most of your customers will open it.
- **Title and description** so the link looks good when shared and Google understands what you sell.

## How I work

1. You tell me what you sell, how many products you have and how customers contact you today.
2. I propose the structure (categories, filters, what data to show) and a style, starting from the demos or your brand.
3. I load the products and we review it together on a phone before publishing.
4. I publish it and hand you the link, ready for your Instagram bio and WhatsApp messages.

## Web catalog, Instagram and WhatsApp

A catalog doesn't replace your social media. Instagram helps people discover you, but finding a specific product and its price among hundreds of posts is slow. The catalog is the link you put in your bio and send when someone asks what you have in stock: customers see everything in one place and message you once they know what they want.
