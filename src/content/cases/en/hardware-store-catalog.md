## What I built

A web catalog with the store's entire inventory, designed to be browsed on a phone:

- **Over 400 products in 9 categories**: processors, motherboards, graphics cards, RAM, storage, power supplies, cooling, cases and controllers.
- **Search and category filters**, to find a specific part without scrolling through the whole list.
- **Prices in US dollars** shown on every product.
- **A cart**, so customers can put together an order with several parts.
- **Customizable PC bundles**, a quick way to sell complete builds.
- **A WhatsApp button** to close the sale over chat.
- **The shop's address and social links**, for people who prefer to buy in person.

## Why it works

A PC parts catalog has lots of products and lots of specs. Organizing it by category and adding search means customers reach WhatsApp knowing what they want, instead of starting the conversation by asking what's in stock.
