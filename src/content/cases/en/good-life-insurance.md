## The problem

Good Life Insurance offered its insurance services without a strong online presence. That had three consequences:

- **They were losing potential customers**: people looking for the company couldn't find a trustworthy place to learn about it.
- **Sign-up was slow and manual**, which held back people who did want to buy.
- **Information about their services was hard to find**, so the same questions kept coming up.

## The solution

Working together with the company's team, I built a professional website that solved those three points:

- A site that presents the company and its services clearly and builds trust with people arriving from social media.
- An **automated sign-up process** that replaced manual registration.
- Service information organized and easy to access, instead of relying on one-to-one messages.

## The results

- The company now has a **professional website**.
- The sign-up process is **automated**.
- **+50% customers** after promoting the site on social media.
- **+40% company growth** following that increase.

## What other businesses can take from this

This case shows something common in small businesses: social media brings visits, but without a site that explains the service well and makes the next step easy, many of those visits are lost. If your business is in a similar spot, a [landing page](/en/services/landing-pages/) or [custom development](/en/services/full-stack-web-development/) with automated sign-up can be the starting point.
