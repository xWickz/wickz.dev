## The goal

Many businesses sell through Instagram and WhatsApp and find it hard to picture what a "web catalog" actually is. Instead of explaining it, I built four working examples anyone can open on their phone. All four are tech shops, because that's a case with many products, specs and prices that change.

## Laptop catalog: the essentials

This is the starting point for a shop that sells on WhatsApp today. Laptops are grouped into categories (Intel, Ryzen, Gaming and Open Box), and each card shows the processor, memory, storage, screen and price. A button leads to WhatsApp to close the sale in a conversation.

## WickzTech: for large catalogs

With many products, finding one becomes the problem. WickzTech adds search by part, brand or spec, category filters (processors, graphics cards, memory, storage, monitors and laptops), sort by price and the option to see only what's in stock.

## WTECH: deals and dark mode

WTECH focuses on promotions: the previous price crossed out next to the sale price, a filter to see only discounted products, and a light and dark mode visitors can choose.

## Wickz Extreme: a full storefront

This is the closest to a large online store: campaign banners on the home page, category shortcuts (gaming PCs, graphics cards, processors, monitors), a selection of featured products and a cart.

## What they show

A web catalog doesn't have a fixed size. It can be a simple list that's quick to set up or a storefront with search and a cart, depending on how many products you have and how you sell.
