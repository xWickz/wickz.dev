## The challenge

A menu with many dishes works well on paper, because you can flip through it. On a phone it's different: if everything is in one long list, customers have to scroll for a long time to find a dish. The menu had to be complete without becoming hard to navigate.

## The solution

- **15 categories** to go straight to what matters: deals, empanadas, arepas, breakfast plates, chicken, red meat, fish, pasta, burgers, Middle Eastern food, sandwiches and delis, salads, snacks, coffee, and cold drinks and infusions.
- **Dish search** that shows a clear message when nothing is found, instead of an empty screen.
- **Deals at the top**, so what the business wants to sell first is the first thing people see.
- **Useful descriptions**, with ingredients and portion sizes (for example, the 150 g shawarma with fresh vegetables and sauces).
- **Visible reference prices** on every dish.

## Why it works

Customers don't have to download anything: they scan the QR code or open the link and they're in the menu. And because the menu lives on a web page, changing a price or adding a dish doesn't mean reprinting anything.
